These WOFF2 files are subsets of the Latin Modern fonts, version 2.004, from CTAN (https://ctan.org/pkg/lm).
They were subset with pyftsubset (fontTools) to Latin plus the symbols the page uses; the unmodified originals are available from CTAN.
